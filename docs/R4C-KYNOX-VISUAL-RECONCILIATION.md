## Decision

The material correction is limited to the Sales Command Center composition and scoped styles: remove the permanent form wall, introduce a compact signal strip and My Work queue, foreground the active Opportunity lifecycle, and move creation into contextual drawers. The hybrid mineral-neutral/graphite palette, teal/amber semantics, RTL-safe identifiers, and restrained motion preserve KYNOX grammar without restyling the public or existing commercial experiences. No parallax or marketing scrollytelling is used for this high-frequency operating screen.
